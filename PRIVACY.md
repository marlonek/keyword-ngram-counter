# Privacy Policy — Keyword n-gram counter

_Last updated: 2026-10-23_

Keyword n-gram counter ("the add-on") is designed to be privacy-preserving by default.

## What data we collect

**None.** The add-on does not collect, store remotely, transmit, sell, or share any
personal data, spreadsheet data, or usage data. There are no analytics, no telemetry,
no tracking, and no cookies.

## How the add-on works

The add-on shows a card in the Google Sheets side panel. You paste text into the card, and
the add-on counts the repeated phrases and shows the result in the same card. The processing
happens inside the add-on from the text you paste; it does not read your spreadsheet and the
result never leaves the card. The add-on makes **no network requests**.

## Permissions

**None.** The add-on requests no OAuth scopes and has no access to your Google account, your
spreadsheets, or any other data. It only processes the text you paste into its card.

## Data sharing

We do not sell or transfer user data to third parties, and we do not use user data for any
purpose unrelated to the add-on's single purpose (counting repeated phrases in your text).

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same location with a
new "Last updated" date.
